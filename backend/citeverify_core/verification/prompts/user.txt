## Task
1. Determine whether the source evidence supports the claim above.
2. Identify whether the claim involves time-sensitive information (e.g. rates, rankings, current state-of-the-art).
3. Check whether specific numbers/statistics in the claim are present in the source.
   NOTE: treat equivalent forms as matching: "30%" == "30 percent", "0.30", "30 out of 100".
   For ranges: a claim of "30%" is supported if the source says "28-32%" or "approximately 30%".
   A missing key figure counts as NOT matching even if other numbers are present.
4. Check whether the scope of the source matches the claim scope (e.g. is a small-pilot finding being
   presented as a general result? is a lab result being applied to clinical outcomes?). If the source
   covers a narrower or different population/context, set scope_mismatch=true.

Respond with a JSON object with these fields:
- verdict        (string enum: supported | partly_supported | unsupported | contradictory | possibly_outdated | needs_review)
- explanation    (string, 1-3 sentences, evidence-based)
- llm_certainty  (float 0-1, your confidence in the verdict)
- supporting_quote (string | null, verbatim quote from the source, or null if none found)
- time_sensitive (bool)
- numbers_match  (bool)
- scope_mismatch (bool)
